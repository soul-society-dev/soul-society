About Soul Society

Interest groups often live in separate WhatsApp chats, which makes them hard to find and easy to miss. Soul Society brings them into one place, where you can explore what you love, join activities and meet other members.

  • Explore societies built around what you enjoy.
  • Take part in games, karaoke nights and reading challenges.
  • Stay informed with public announcements and an events calendar.
